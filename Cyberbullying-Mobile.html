02 Definition

What is cyberbullying?

Using phones, apps, social media or games to harass, threaten, humiliate or exclude another person — usually again and again.

> It's not "just online". The hurt is real.

Repeated

A pattern that keeps coming back — or one post shared over and over.

Intentional

Done on purpose to hurt, scare or shame.

Power imbalance

The target finds it hard to defend themselves.

04 Threat catalogue

Forms of cyberbullying

Harassment

Repeated insulting or threatening messages.

Flaming

Heated online fights full of angry, vulgar language.

Outing & doxxing

Exposing secrets or personal info publicly.

Exclusion

Deliberately shutting someone out of groups and chats.

Impersonation

Fake or hacked accounts posting as you.

Cyberstalking

Tracking, monitoring and intimidating someone.

Trolling

Provoking and mocking just to get a reaction.

Image abuse

Edited, fake or private photos shared to humiliate.

08 Damage report

The impact on victims

Mind

Anxiety, depression, shame and low self-worth.

Body

Sleep problems, headaches, stomach aches.

School

Skipping class, losing focus, falling grades.

Social

Isolation, withdrawal, losing trust in friends.

In the most serious cases it can lead to self-harm. That's why speaking up matters.

10 Threat actor profile

Why do people cyberbully?

"It's just a joke"

They don't see the harm behind the screen.

Anonymity

Feeling invisible makes people crueller.

Peer pressure

Joining in to fit in with a group.

Revenge

Hitting back after being hurt themselves.

Attention

Chasing likes, reactions and drama.

Empathy gap

No face, no tears — no instant feedback.

11 The bystander effect

Silence amplifies

Most people who see cyberbullying are bystanders. Every like, share or laugh tells the bully it's working.

Don't like or share
Check in privately
Report the content
Tell a trusted adult

12 Response protocol

If it's happening to you

01

Don't reply

Hitting back only fuels it.

02

Screenshot

Save messages, dates and usernames.

03

Block

Cut off their access to you.

04

Report

Use the platform's report tools.

05

Tell someone

A parent, teacher or counsellor.

06

Recharge

Log off. Your wellbeing comes first.

It is NOT your fault.
